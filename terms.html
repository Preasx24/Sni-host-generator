By listing an item, you confirm that you are the legal owner and have the right to sell or rent it. Fake, stolen, or prohibited items are strictly banned and may be reported to authorities.

Student Hub acts as a middleman between sellers and customers. We manage the listing, communication, transactions, and delivery/handover process.

  • Buyers and renters transact directly with Student Hub.
  • We pay sellers/renters after the item has been successfully handed over or delivered.

6. Payments

  • After the item is delivered or picked up by the buyer/renter
